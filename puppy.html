Canidae-Puppies!

Canidae is a family including wolves, dogs, foxes, coyotes, and jackals. I love all of these animals! It's hard to say what i like about them most because what's not to like about them? Their fluffy, wag their tails, and have super cute ears. My favorite dog breeds are Bedlington terriers and Wire fox terriers. Fun fact:I know practically every AKC recognized dog breed!

  • Habitat: Dependant on species, houses, forests, desserts, arctic tundra
  • Dietary Classification: mesocarnivores-hypercarnivores
  • Lifespan: 8-15y (domestic dog)
  • 3-10y (wild) up to 20y captive
  • Love: Most species form long-term mating pairs. Only the most dominate pair in a pack breeds and the rest help care for the puppies.
  • Speed: Up to 30-45 mph
