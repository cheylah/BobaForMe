Phocidae-Seals

Phocidae is the family of earless seals (superior to eared seals). Eared seals are big and ugly but earless are the complete opposite! Harp seals/saddleback seals are my favorite. Watching videos of seal pups flopping around is very entertaining. There are tons of super cute harp seals in sanctuaries I would love to visit one day.

  • Habitat: Polar and marine
  • Dietary Classification: Carnivores or piscivores
  • Lifespan: 20-35y
  • Love: The biggest males fight for the love of groups of ladies on the beach. The males are very loud and dominate but ladies are known for devoting themselves to the care of their pups.
  • Speed: Swim 3-6 mph but can burst up to 25 mph for short amounts of time.
