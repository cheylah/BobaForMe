Cygnus-Swans

The family of cygnus is just swans. My favorite species of swans is definitely black swans or mute swans. Mute swans are the classic swans that's why I love them so much. What i love most about black swans is their curly feathers. Swans are such romantic creatures, they mate for life and guard their nests ferocious. I've loved swans since i was a toddler reading the ugly duckling (I reread it like 100 times). I believe literature tends to affect me greatly in this way (that is shaping what I love most as well as they way I behave).

  • Habitat: lowland freshwater wetlands
  • Dietary Classification: omnivores
  • Lifespan: 10-20y wild 30-40y captive/protected
  • Love: Swans are renowned as symbols of romance. They devote themselves to their partner for live and have many bonding rituals.
  • Speed: water-2.5-6 mph land- 10-22 mph sky- 30-50 mph
