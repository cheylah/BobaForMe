Strigiformes-Owls

Strigiformes is a order split into two familys, Tytonidae and Strigidae. Strigidae are typical owls with round faces like Great horned owls. Tytonidae are barn owls, they are described as having heart shaped faces. I’m not sure what family I like better, both are cool! I like how quite they are and their huge eyes are adorable.

  • Habitat: Varies! From hollowed out trees to abandoned buildings.
  • Dietary Classification: Carnivores or insectivores for some species
  • Lifespan: 1-20y depending heavily on size of species
  • Love: Males have adorable courtship rituals. They mate for life but may split up. During the day they are usually solitary.
  • Speed: Top speeds up to 50 mph! And some species can dive up to 120 mph!
