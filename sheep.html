Caprinae-Lambs/Kids

Caprinae is a sub-family including ovis (sheep), capra (goats), and goat-antelopes. I just like sheep and goats though. They are very funny when you interact with them but are also very smart. Flocks are so awesome and I kind of hope to own a small flock when im older. My dad has a farm with lots of goats and sheep in Benin. They are cuties.

  • Habitat: Dependant on species, grasslands , alpine regions, desserts, mountains

  • Lifespan: 10-18y
  • Love: like deer Caprinae males fight for love during the rut by clashing horns. They attempt to get the lady's attention by smell, singing, and odd dances.
